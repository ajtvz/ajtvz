## `whoami`

```text
AJ — Developer in progress, problem solver by nature.
```

I'm a self-motivated developer who believes the best way to grow is to stay curious, build things, break things, and figure out why they broke.

My journey started with HTML and CSS, grew through the #100Devs community, and continues with new technologies, side projects, and the occasional late-night debugging session.

I bring a military background, a team-first mindset, and a commitment to getting a little better every day. I'm here to create useful things, learn from people smarter than me, and leave every project better than I found it.

- ⚡ **Current mission:** Learn, build, ship, repeat.
- 🧠 **Mindset:** Stay curious. Embrace the challenge.
- 🛠️ **Interests:** Software development, web technologies, automation, and creative problem-solving.
- 🤝 **Open to:** Collaborations, interesting projects, and connecting with fellow developers.
- 🎮 **Off the clock:** Gaming, tech, and exploring what's possible.
